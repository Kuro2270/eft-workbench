General

Is it free? Do I need an account?

Everything is free and there is no sign-up. Just open it in your browser.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. On pages with wide tables, scroll sideways to see every column.

How fresh are the prices?

Each tool shows “Prices as of …” next to its title. Prices refresh about once an hour, and an open page swaps in the new prices on its own.

Are PvP and PvE prices different?

Yes. Use the switch at the top left to pick PvP (regular), PvP seasonal or PvE, and everything is recalculated for that mode.

Are my settings and stock saved?

They are saved in your browser, so they are still there next time. They do not carry over to other devices or browsers.

Can I use it in Japanese?

Yes. The tool pages switch between English and Japanese at the top right.

Flip board

What should I set up first?

In Player settings, enter your player level, trader loyalty levels and Intelligence Center level. Fees and what you can buy are then calculated for your situation.

Why don’t I make the “Profit / item” shown?

The cheapest offer is often bought by someone else first. Items with few offers or big price swings are harder to buy or sell at the listed price. Check the profitable share and sellability as well.

What are “Profitable share” and “Expected profit”?

They come from the price history of the top items over the period you choose. Profitable share is how much of that time the trade cleared your minimum profit. Expected profit is that share multiplied by the average profit when it did.

Anything to watch when buying from traders to sell on the flea?

Selling on the flea needs a minimum player level. And when the flea only accepts found-in-raid (FIR) items, items bought from traders may not be listable; a notice appears above the table when that is the case.

Loot tier list

How are the tiers (S to D) decided?

Items are ranked by value per slot (the better of the trader price and the flea take-home) and split into tiers. Small, valuable items rank above big, cheap ones.

Why are some items missing?

Categories you untick and items with too few flea offers are left out. You can change both at the top of the page.

Buy & sell signals

What are the buy zone and sell zone?

Items whose current price sits near the low of the period you choose (7, 14 or 30 days) are in the buy zone, and items near the high are in the sell zone. Select an item to see its price and moving-average chart with suggested buy and sell prices.

What is “Stock” for?

Press “Bought” on a buy-zone item and enter the quantity and cost. The stock list then shows your profit or loss if you sold now, and whether it is time to sell. Stock is kept per mode, in your browser only.

Rat spots

What do the four ratings mean?

View (how much you can see from the spot), Sound (how easily you hear footsteps coming), Scav-safe (how unlikely Scavs are to find you) and Easy to reach (how safely you get there). Each is rated from 1 to 5.

Will more spots be added?

Yes, a few at a time for each map. The videos are also on the YouTube channel “Rat of Tarkov”.
