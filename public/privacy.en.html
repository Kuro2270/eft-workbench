Where the data comes from

Item prices and details come from tarkov.dev, and this site does its own calculations on top of them.

An unofficial fan site

This is an unofficial fan site and is not affiliated with Battlestate Games, the developer of Escape from Tarkov. Item names, images and other game content belong to Battlestate Games.

What is stored in your browser

Your player settings (level, trader loyalty levels, Intelligence Center and so on), display settings, and the items and quantities you record as stock in Buy & sell signals are saved in your browser’s local storage, so everything is the same next time you open the site.

None of this is sent to anyone, including the operator. You can delete it from your browser settings.

Loading data from other sites

To show price history, or when this site has no saved prices, your browser loads data directly from tarkov.dev. Your connection details, such as your IP address, are then sent to tarkov.dev and handled under its policies.

Embedded videos

The Rat spots page embeds YouTube videos. A video is loaded from YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) only when you press its play button. After that, connection details are sent to YouTube and handled under Google’s privacy policy.

Analytics

To improve the site, visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It uses no cookies and collects nothing that identifies you.

Contact

The contact form uses Google Forms. What you enter (your message and, if you choose, your email address) is stored on Google’s service and read by the operator. It is used only to reply, to check reported bugs and to improve the site, and is never given to third parties without your consent, except where required by law.

Disclaimer

Prices and results on this site are not guaranteed to be accurate. The operator is not responsible for any loss arising from use of the site, including in-game losses.

Changes

This policy may change without notice. If the information handled changes, for example if ads are added, this page will be updated.
