Before you send

For bug reports, these details help us check quickly:

  • Which page (Flip board, Loot tier list, …)
  • Your device and browser (e.g. Chrome on Windows)
  • The mode (PvP or PvE) and what happened

About replies

The site is run by one person, so replies can take a while, and some messages may not get a reply. If you would like an answer, enter your email address in the form. What you send is handled as described in the privacy policy.
