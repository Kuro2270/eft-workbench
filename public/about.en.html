About

EFT Workbench is a free set of tools for Escape from Tarkov. It uses flea market prices to help you decide what to buy, what to sell and what to bring back, and it also shares rat spots where ambushes win fights.

Why this site

Flea and trader prices keep moving. Working out what pays once fees, buy limits and loyalty levels are included is a chore between raids. This site does that math for you and turns it into lists you can act on the moment you open them. It is all free, with no sign-up.

What you can do

Flip boardLists items you can profit from, using the price gap between the flea market and traders.
  • Three ways: buy on flea and sell to traders, buy from traders and sell on flea, barter at traders and sell on flea
  • Fees, buy limits and loyalty levels are included
  • For the top items, past prices show the profitable share and how easily they sell
Loot tier listSorts what you find in raid into tiers S to D by value per slot.
  • Uses the better of the trader price and the flea take-home (after fees)
  • Ammo boxes are valued by their contents when that pays more
  • Filter by category and number of offers
Buy & sell signalsRanks items priced near their past low (buy zone) and near their past high (sell zone).
  • Suggested buy and sell prices, with a price and moving-average chart
  • Record what you bought as stock and watch for the moment to sell
Rat spotsAmbush (rat) spots that win fights, map by map.
  • Position on the map, route and the view from the spot
  • Ratings for view, sound, Scav safety and how easy it is to reach
  • Real kill videos
Player settingsWhere you enter your player level, trader loyalty levels, Intelligence Center and more.
  • Shared by every tool
  • Used for fees and for what you can buy or sell on the flea

How to use it

  1. Enter your situationIn Player settings, enter your level, loyalty levels and Intelligence Center (just once).
  2. Pick a modeAt the top left, choose PvP (regular), PvP seasonal or PvE.
  3. Open a toolThe flip board to make money, the loot tier list during raids, and buy & sell signals to watch the market.
